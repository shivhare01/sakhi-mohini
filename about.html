About Sakhi Mohini

Celebration clothes, chosen with heart.

We bring together Indian occasionwear for the moments that call for color, detail and a little extra joy.

Our point of view

Sakhi Mohini is rooted in the belief that getting dressed for a celebration should feel exciting and easy. Our edit is made for weddings, festivals, family gatherings and every meaningful moment in between.

We look for pieces with character—rich color, thoughtful finishes and silhouettes that help you feel like yourself. The result is a collection created around real celebrations and the people who make them memorable.

For every invitation

Whether you are choosing a statement lehenga, a festive vest or a piece to gift someone special, we want the experience to feel personal. Browse the collection, explore the details and contact us whenever you would like a hand choosing.
