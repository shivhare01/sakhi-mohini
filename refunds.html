Returns & refunds

We want you to feel good about your piece.

If something is not right, reach out soon after delivery and we will guide you through the next step.

Starting a return request

Please contact us within 14 days of delivery and include your order details plus a short note about the issue. We will review the request and send the appropriate return instructions.

Item condition

To be considered for return, items should be unused, unworn, unwashed and returned with their original tags and packaging. We may be unable to accept items that arrive in a condition different from when they were sent.

Refund timing

When a return is approved and received, we will confirm the outcome by email. Any approved refund is issued to the original card payment method. Your bank or card issuer may need additional time to post the credit.

Damaged or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged or is not the item you ordered, contact us promptly with your order details and photos if possible. We will work with you to make it right.
