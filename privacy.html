Privacy

Your details stay connected to your order.

We use the information you share to help you browse, purchase and receive your Sakhi Mohini pieces.

Information we use

When you contact us or make a purchase, you may provide information such as your name, email address, delivery address and order details. We use that information to respond to you, calculate delivery options, process the order and provide customer support.

Payments

Card payments are processed through Stripe’s secure checkout. Sakhi Mohini does not collect or store your complete card number on this website. If you choose Zelle, your bank handles that payment through its own service.

How information is shared

We share only the details needed with service providers that help process payment or ship an order, such as payment and delivery partners. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can contact us to ask about the information connected with an inquiry or order. If you choose to join our email updates, you can opt out at any time.

Questions about privacy? Get in touch with us.
