Shipping

Clear delivery choices, before you pay.

We show current carrier options during checkout so you can choose the delivery service that works best for you.

Where we deliver

Sakhi Mohini currently ships within the United States. We are not able to offer international delivery at this time.

Choosing your delivery service

On each product page, enter your U.S. delivery address to view current USPS and UPS options. Rates are calculated using the packed item weight and your destination. Choose the option you prefer before beginning secure checkout.

Shipping cost and timing

The delivery price and estimated timing shown on the product page come directly from the carrier. Your selected delivery option and charge are shown again at secure checkout before payment is completed.

Need help with an address or a delivery question? Contact Sakhi Mohini before placing your order.
